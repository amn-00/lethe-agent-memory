lethe

An agent that decides what to remember, what to archive, and when to bring something back.

Tell it a few things, chat for a while, then ask.

Facts you share are kept at the surface of its memory. Small talk is dropped. When memory is full, the least useful facts sink to the archive, and they come back up if you ask about them.
